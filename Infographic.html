For our infographic project, we worked alone to create something that would demonstrate the parts of a type of communication and use different resources to determine the origin, principal of operation, uses, impacts, and possible future pans or uses for your chosen form of communication. I did my infographic on the Citizens Band Radio because my father and grandfather were both truck drivers and often used these radios to communicate between semis on the road.
